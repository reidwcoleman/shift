HOW IT'S MADE

MADE LIKE IT'S 2002.

Every graphic starts as hand-finished illustration, gets separated into three or four spot colors, then aged before it ever touches cotton.

01

DRAWN

Illustration first. Tigers, moths, owls, rally cars — drawn in the flat-color style of 90s tattoo flash and college tees.

02

SEPARATED

Type is set in outlined Anton, Bebas and blackletter, locked into a 12×14 in or 13×16 in print, three to four spot colors.

03

WORN IN

Cracks, fade patches and ink dropout are baked into the file, so day one looks like year twenty. Wash cold, hang dry.

THE STORY

NIGHT SHIFT DIVISION.

Nobody claps for the hours between midnight and five. That's the shift.

SHIFT started in 2002 as a joke between friends pulling all-nighters — "you're on the night shift." It stopped being a joke. Every drop is a punch card: a small run, a hard close, and then it's gone. If you own it, you were there.
